# Carom

Find your angle. Bend the rally. Carom is a quick, top-down paddle duel where a
well-timed slide can turn a straight return into a curling shot. Take on the
computer in Solo, or challenge a friend in local Versus.

The opening serve gives you a moment to settle in. Then each paddle hit adds
speed, the two amber obstacles turn and sway, and the path across the field keeps
changing. Aim for an opening or play the tilted faces for an unexpected bank.

Move your paddle as you strike to put spin on the ball. A rising return can bend
through the middle; a downward stroke can send the next shot sweeping toward a
wall. Read the trail, watch the obstacles, and stay ready for the ricochet.

First to eleven takes the match, but you have to lead by two. At ten-all, every
angle matters. Reset your position, find the next opening, and make match point
count.
